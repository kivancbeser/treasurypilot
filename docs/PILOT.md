# Design-partner pilot invitation

Draft only. No messages have been sent and no pilot team has accepted.

## Message to a potential team

Hi — I'm Kivanc, building TreasuryPilot for early-stage onchain teams. It helps organize revenue allocations, contributor payment requests, separate approvals, and spending reports.

I've shipped a public simulation demo and an authenticated local beta. I'm looking for a team willing to walk through its current treasury process and test one workflow with simulated data. No funds, private keys, or confidential treasury records are needed.

Would you be open to a 20-minute walkthrough? I'd like to understand how you handle budgets and payment approvals, then agree on a small pilot only if it fits.

Demo: https://treasurypilot-gamma.vercel.app
Source: https://github.com/kivancbeser/treasurypilot

## Evidence to record after consent

- Team name and permission to identify it in an application.
- Date, participant role, current workflow and problem described.
- Explicit agreement to participate, agreed scope and timeframe.
- Sessions actually completed, payment requests tested, feedback and decision to continue.
- Any payment received, separately from non-binding interest or a free trial.

A reply expressing interest is not an active user. A test session is not recurring usage. No invented testimonials, partnerships or revenue claims.
